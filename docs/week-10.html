The Worst Team Wins, and the Name Has Never Been More Wrong

Tyler's squad survives J-M-P-S JMPS, JMPS, JMPS! 163.92-153.66 as Joe's bench quietly outscores the margin

Welcome back to the Week 10 highlight show. A team called "The worst team" just beat J-M-P-S JMPS, JMPS, JMPS! by 10.26 points, and the loser had the winning points sitting on its own bench. We'll get to that.

The worst team (Tyler Bolme) cruised past its 120.52 projection by 43.4 points, and Jahmyr Gibbs did most of the lifting. He put up 44.2 against a 23.86 projection, which is the kind of night that makes a coach feel like a genius. Emmett Johnson added 35.1 and Dak Prescott chipped in 34.42. That trio alone accounted for 113.72 points, so the rest of the lineup was basically along for the ride.

Some of it was riding in the back seat with the windows up. Jalen Coker gave Tyler 0.3 points, Dominic Zvada kicked his way to 2.0, and the Jaguars D/ST managed 3.0. Cameron Dicker dropped 11.0 on the bench while Zvada took the K slot. That's a 9.0-point gift to the scoreboard gods, and Tyler's best possible score was 172.92. He won anyway, but the front office is on notice.

Over on the other sideline, Joe Walsh's team did plenty right. Brock Purdy hit 31.66, Isaiah Likely gave the TE slot 27.7, and Cam Little kicked for 19.0, the best kicker line in the league this week. J-M-P-S beat its own projection by 34.38 points and still lost. That's what happens when the other guy has Gibbs having a career night.

Here's the turning point. Joe left Patrick Mahomes (35.42) on the bench behind Purdy, a 3.76-point swap. He also started Chris Olave (6.0) over Malik Washington (13.36), a 7.36-point swap. Add those up and you get 11.12 points, which is more than the 10.26 margin. A source close to the situation says that's called "losing on the waiver wire AND the lineup page." Joe has also made 25 moves this season, the most in the league, and somehow the best one was sitting in his own bench slots.

The stakes: Tyler improves to 5-5, sits 7th and rides a W1 streak, with playoff odds right around 49.5%. Joe drops to 4-6, falls to 9th with an L1 streak, and sees his playoff odds sink to 17.2%. The win keeps Tyler's season alive. The loss leaves Joe checking the math.

Top Play: Jahmyr Gibbs, 44.2 points (projected 23.86). He nearly doubled his projection and was the second-highest scorer in the entire league this week.

Not Top 10: Joe's bench. Mahomes (35.42) and Malik Washington (13.36) sat while Purdy and Olave (6.0) started, and the swaps would have cost Tyler the win. Honorable mention goes to Tyler for starting Zvada (2.0) over Dicker (11.0) and still escaping.

Postgame Pressers:

"We won by 10 and left 9 on the bench. Honestly, we could have beaten them even worse. Don't tell Coker he scored 0.3." (a team source for The worst team)

"We beat our projection by 34 points and still lost. Nobody in the building knows how to feel about Mahomes right now." (a team source for J-M-P-S JMPS, JMPS, JMPS!)
