Josh's Scary Team Leaves 33 Points on the Bench, Still Beats "The Worst Team"

Josh's Scary Team 121.76, The worst team 108.9: A Win So Sloppy the Scoreboard Wants a Recount

Welcome to the highlight desk, where Josh's Scary Team survived Week 9 despite doing everything it could to avoid winning. Josh Addington's squad beat Tyler Bolme's The worst team by 12.86 points. The projections had this going the other way, with Tyler at 129.34 and Josh at 120.7. Josh cleared his number by just 1.06.

The hero was CeeDee Lamb, who dropped 33.5 against a projection of 21.22. That is more than Tyler's best starter, Dak Prescott, put up (19.5). Then the kickers crashed the party. Jake Bates scored 15.0 and finished ahead of starting QB Jayden Daniels (14.56). The Chiefs D/ST added 12.0, and Parker Washington chipped in 14.1. When your kicker is your second-best player, you are riding the wave, not steering it.

Now the crime scene. Josh started Daniels at QB and left Jordan Love on the bench, where he scored 36.18. That would have been the second-highest individual score in the entire league this week. That's a 21.62-point swing, and it's why Josh left 33.22 on the pine. A perfect lineup would have scored 154.98. Zay Flowers (5.3) in the flex spot, Kyren Williams (9.1) and Saquon Barkley (8.2) did not help either.

Tyler's day was quieter and sadder. Jahmyr Gibbs managed 13.4 against a projection of 26.54, a 13-point hole. Dominic Zvada, his kicker, scored 17.0 and outscored Gibbs, and he was Tyler's second-best player. Blake Corum (11.8) sat on the bench while Tyler Allgeier posted 6.6. The good news for Tyler is that his best possible lineup was 114.3, so no lineup tweak would have saved him. Sources say that is not the comfort it sounds like.

The standings fallout: Josh moves to 5-4 on a three-game win streak, sitting 4th with a 68.0% playoff probability. Tyler slides to 4-5 and drops to 8th with a 38.2% shot, ending on an L1. The name says "worst," but he's technically 8th, so Jose, Darren and Danny are happy to hold the actual title.

Top Play: CeeDee Lamb, 33.5 points for Josh's Scary Team. He beat his projection by 12.28 and outscored every starter on the other side, and he is why Josh's lineup mistakes didn't cost him the game.

Not Top 10: Josh benching Jordan Love (36.18) for Jayden Daniels (14.56). That's 21.62 points left on the bench at QB alone, and the full bench total was 33.22. Josh won, but a win this ugly should come with an apology to Love.

Postgame Pressers:

"We showed up with a 129.34 projection and left with a 108.9 and a kicker who outscored our first-round RB," said a team source for The worst team. "At least the name is still technically inaccurate."
