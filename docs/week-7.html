Pukachu Stays Perfect by Surviving Its Own Lineup

Steve's 7-0 juggernaut limps past Team Name? 107.42-102.18 while both sides blow their projections by 25+ points

Good evening, and welcome to the Week 7 game that was closer than a 7-0 team should ever allow. "Pukachu, I Choose You" scored 107.42 to beat "Team Name?" by 5.24 points. That's the closest finish of the week, and the unbeaten club is still unbeaten.

Pukachu was projected for 132.22 and landed 24.8 points short. Team Name? was projected for 130.10 and missed by 27.92. Neither team showed up to the projection party, but one of them still has a perfect record.

The star was Bucky Irving, who dropped 24.0 against a projection of 14.11. Deshaun Watson added 18.22 at QB, Bijan Robinson chipped in 17.2, and George Kittle brought 14.8. Then came the back end of the lineup. Nico Collins posted 2.6 points, and kicker Ryan Fitzgerald posted a single point. One. Steve's front office left 6.2 points on the bench, with Jaylen Waddle (8.8) sitting there while Collins did his best impression of a decoy.

Over on the other sideline, Hannah's squad had its own highlights. Puka Nacua, who has the wrong team but the right name, put up 21.8 from the flex spot. Brock Bowers added 13.3, Aaron Rodgers 13.08, and kicker Chris Boswell a sturdy 11.0. Then the decisions started. Alvin Kamara scored 13.2 on the bench while Kendre Miller put up 5.8 in the RB slot. That's a 7.4-point swing in a game decided by 5.24. Do the math, Hannah. Please.

In all, Team Name? left 13.3 points on the pine, and its best possible score of 115.48 would have beaten Pukachu's actual 107.42. We can't prove anything, but we're calling it a crime scene.

Pukachu moves to 7-0 on a seven-game winning streak, with 994.08 points scored (best in the league) and a 99.65% shot at the playoffs. Team Name? falls to 3-4 with a one-game losing streak, sits 6th, and holds playoff odds of about 47.2%. That's a coin flip with a worse haircut.

Top Play: Bucky Irving, RB, Pukachu: 24.0 points against a 14.11 projection. He carried a team that scored 2.6 from a starting WR and 1.0 from its kicker.

Not Top 10: Starting Kendre Miller (5.8) over Alvin Kamara (13.2) in a game lost by 5.24. That's a 7.4-point gift to the unbeaten team, wrapped and delivered. Honorable mention goes to Steve's 1.0-point kicker, who nearly turned a win into a group project.

Not Top 10: Darren benching KC Concepcion (23.2) for Marvin Harrison Jr. (7.6) in the flex. Swap them and The Backup PMD wins by roughly 3 points. Runner-up: Lynn starting a -1.0 Patriots D/ST and a 5.9-point Henry while Judkins dropped 23.4 on the bench.
