* Roast decisions and luck: bad lineups, benched studs, panic drops,
zero-point starters, losing streaks, kickers outscoring stars.
* Every team gets ribbed eventually, including the commissioner's team
and Joe's own team (JMPS). Nobody is safe, nobody gets piled on.
* Keep it to football. Never jokes about anyone's real-life job, family,
looks, money, relationships, or anything personal.
